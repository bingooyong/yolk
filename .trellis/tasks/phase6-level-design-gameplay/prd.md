# PRD — Phase 6 Level Design & Gameplay

## Problem

Eight themed courses exist. They are obstacle strips, not races. Level 1 can be finished by holding forward. Jump / Pounce / Roll / Boost have no space problem that requires them.

## Goal

Turn Level 1 (糖果草原) into the gameplay gold standard: teach the four moves through the course, offer a shortcut and a recovery, checkpoint after challenges, first-clear about 45–90s.

## In

Audit of all eight (design identity only for 2–8). Level 1 layout, blockout, playtest, lock, existing-style art pass, session stats, level skills, trellis spec.

## Out

Rebuild of levels 2–8. Rapier / Action Pad / gacha / skin pipeline. Bot personality rewrite. TrackSegment compiler. Live tuning panel. Server telemetry.
