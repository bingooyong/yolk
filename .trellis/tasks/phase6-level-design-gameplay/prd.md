# PRD — Phase 6 Level Design & Gameplay

## Problem

Eight themed courses exist. They are obstacle strips, not races. Level 1 can be finished by holding forward. Jump / Pounce / Roll / Boost have no space problem that requires them.

## Goal

Turn teaching courses into gameplay gold standards: Level 1 (糖果草原) teaches the four moves; Level 2 (冰雪滑坡) is ice steering; Level 3 (旋转工厂) is hammer wait-windows; Level 4 (天空弹跳岛) is bounce-and-choose-a-landing. First-clear about 45–90s. Shortcut + recovery. Checkpoint after challenges.

## In

Audit of all eight (design identity only for 5–8). Meadow, ice, factory, and sky layout, blockout, playtest, lock, existing-style art pass, session stats, level skills, trellis spec.

## Out

Rebuild of levels 5–8. Rapier / Action Pad / gacha / skin pipeline. Bot personality rewrite. TrackSegment compiler. Live tuning panel. Server telemetry.
