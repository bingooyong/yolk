# Skin art style

Yolk Rush characters read as round, readable silhouettes with PBR materials, a face, and one or two secondary forms (ears, armor, cape). Do not hide low mesh detail with bloom.

Full characters must fit the existing yolk capsule: overall height ~1.2, width ~1.1. Fur / metal / plastic are roughness and metalness, not extra physics.

Original IPs only. No licensed names, logos, or lookalikes.
