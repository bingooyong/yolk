# Yolk Rush P0 abilities

Keyboard: Space/K jump · I/F pounce · L roll · J/Shift boost (hold to charge) · Q/E look · G grab (reserved)
Gamepad: A jump · B pounce · X roll · RT/RB boost · right stick look

| Ability | Duration | Speed | Cooldown | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jump | coyote/buffer | — | none | grounded / coyote only |
| Pounce | 0.22s | 14.4 | 0.82s | short lunge, visual lean, not a dash |
| Roll | 0.32s | 11.8 | 0.70s | visual spin only; physics stays upright |
| Boost | 0.16–0.36s | 12.2–20.2 | 0.95s | hold to charge 3 levels |
| Grab | — | — | — | P1, locked |

Exclusive: pounce / roll / boost-active cannot overlap. Roll may jump. Pounce cannot re-pounce.

Camera look is independent. Looking back (>100°) does not invert move. Recenter after 1.7s idle.
Player marker: billboard arrow over the local egg only.

Playtest notes (2026-08-28): meadow still completable with W; boost release hits ~20; pounce/roll are shorter bursts.
